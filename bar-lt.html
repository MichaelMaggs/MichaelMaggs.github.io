BAR-LT

Professional two-channel recording in the field.

BAR-LT is designed for research teams that need a rugged professional recorder with precise scheduling, flexible power options, and dependable field performance.

Overview

Built to meet stringent professional standards, BAR-LT provides full-size recorder capability with strong recording capacity, true lossless compression, and configurable microphone layouts.

With GPS time-stamping, sophisticated schedule support, and external or battery power options, it is well suited to research deployments that need dependable, repeatable acoustic capture.

At a glance

  • Professional full-size 2-channel recorder
  • GPS time-stamping and geolocation
  • Flexible scheduling with sunrise/sunset triggers
  • External power or rechargeable battery operation
  • Highly sensitive low-noise microphones
