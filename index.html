FAQ

Common questions from research and field teams

Which recorder is best for a compact biodiversity survey?

BAR-V2 is the most compact and affordable option, with large recording capacity and the flexibility to support many field deployments.

Which system is ideal for long-term remote monitoring?

Solar-BAR is built specifically for remote deployments that need solar charging and long endurance across extended field seasons.

Is BAR-LT suitable for professional research setups?

Yes. BAR-LT is designed for more advanced field requirements, including two-channel capture, flexible power arrangements, and rigorous scheduling.
