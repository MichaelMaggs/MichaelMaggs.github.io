Solar-BAR

Ultra-endurance monitoring powered by the sun.

Solar-BAR is built for remote and difficult environments where a recorder must operate for months at a time with minimal maintenance and maximum reliability.

Overview

The Solar-BAR is designed to support continuous passive monitoring in challenging settings, combining a top-mounted solar panel, weather-resistant enclosure, and long-duration battery system.

This makes it suitable for deployments where site access is limited and consistent monitoring is essential across long field seasons.

At a glance

  • Top-mounted 10W monocrystalline solar panel
  • 18+ months before memory card changes
  • 1000 hours internal battery reserve
  • Internal 12V sealed lead acid battery
  • Rugged lockable UV-resistant case

How long can Solar-BAR operate in the field?

It is designed for ultra-endurance monitoring and can continue for lengthy periods before card changes are required.

Is the solar panel adjustable?

Yes. The top-mounted panel includes pivoted mounting to support optimal positioning for charging.
