BAR-V2

Compact, rugged, and ready for the field.

The BAR-V2 brings together compact design, long recording duration, and GPS-enabled reliability in a recorder made for biodiversity and ecological surveys.

Overview

BAR-V2 is the latest and most compact recorder in the Frontier Labs range, designed to deliver a no-compromise monitoring experience in a smaller and more affordable form factor.

The system is built for long-duration field deployments, with rugged waterproof housing, stackable design, and configurable sampling for research, nocturnal monitoring, and habitat surveys.

At a glance

  • Compact and lightweight body
  • Up to 800 hours recording with battery pack
  • 16-bit WAV and FLAC support
  • GPS time-sync and geolocation
  • Lockable, weather-resistant enclosure

Key features

Built for reliable acoustic monitoring

High-quality capture

Two ultra-low-noise omnidirectional microphones with adjustable gain and sample rates from 8 kHz to 96 kHz.

Flexible storage

One SD card slot supports up to 1TB, making it suitable for longer deployment windows.

Smart scheduling

Custom schedules support sunrise/sunset triggers and future wake-up timing for efficient power usage.

Research workflows

Built-in GPS and filter options allow precise capture and later analysis of acoustic environments.

What makes BAR-V2 different from BAR-LT?

BAR-V2 is smaller, lighter, and more compact while still delivering high-quality audio capture and long deployment performance.

Does it support time syncing?

Yes. BAR-V2 includes built-in GPS for automatic time syncing and geolocation stamping.
